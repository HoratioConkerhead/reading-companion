# Stitched Up v2: chapter corrections

Events, objects and mysteries in v2 were assigned chapters in sequence (two events per chapter: Preface, 1, 1, 2, 2, ... 24, 24) rather than where they happen in the text, so the chapter filter showed late events, including the assassination attempt and Louise's double role, to readers early in the book.

Each item now belongs to the **earliest chapter by which everything its description says is known**. Usually that is where it happens; where a description gives away something learned later (e.g. what the suitcase contained), it is placed at that later chapter instead, so it can't spoil it. Chapter numbers were checked against the text in `books/Matt Parry - Stitched Up/` (Chapters 44 to 50 and the Epilogue were read in full; earlier chapters via the chapter summaries and searches of the text).

Only chapter fields changed: no names, descriptions or other data.

## Notes for v4

- **Descriptions that mention later events** push items later than where they first appear: *Cynthia's House* ("later taken over by the Harringtons", so now Ch 48), *Mysterious Suitcase* (its contents, Ch 36) and *American Transport Division Camp* ("becoming the target", Ch 45). Rewording these to describe only what is known when they first appear would let them show earlier.
- **Events with descriptions that don't match the text:**
  - *Flight to Ireland* says "Davies, Davies and Snowdens flee to Ireland ... leaving the Harringtons to take over". In Ch 48 it is the Snowdens and Megan who have gone; Sir John comes to Cynthia's house with the Harringtons and is arrested in Ch 50 (as *Arrests and Capture* says).
  - *Cheque Book Discovery* says the cheque book is found; in the text it goes missing from the flat (Ch 30) and is later used to pay for the ferry to Ireland (Ch 47).
- **Chapter summaries 44 to 50** in `chapters.js` don't follow the text closely (e.g. Ch 44's summary mentions Louise's marksmanship, which isn't in that chapter). They read like they were written without the text; worth regenerating in v4.
- **`timeline.js`** has its own `chapter` fields using the same old sequence, but the app doesn't use it, so it was left unchanged.
- **Events per chapter**: many chapters still have no events (e.g. Chapters 4 to 7, 24, 26 and 27). v4's per-chapter extraction should fill these in.
